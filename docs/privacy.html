Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 30, 2025

The short version

Your data stays on your Mac. Quip doesn't collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information or text expansions on external servers.

What data does Quip access?

Quip monitors your keystrokes solely to detect trigger patterns (like ;shortcut) and expand them into your saved text. This monitoring happens entirely on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

What data does Quip store?

Your text expansions (triggers and their expanded text) are stored locally on your Mac using Apple's secure data storage. This data never leaves your device.

What data does Quip collect?

None. Quip does not collect analytics, usage data, crash reports, or any other information. We have no servers, no accounts, and no way to access your data.

Third-party services

Quip does not integrate with any third-party services or APIs. Everything runs locally on your Mac.

Accessibility permissions

Quip requires Accessibility permissions to monitor keystrokes and perform text expansion. This permission is used solely for the app's core functionality and nothing else.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how Quip handles data (for example, adding cloud sync), we'll update this policy and notify users before any changes take effect.
